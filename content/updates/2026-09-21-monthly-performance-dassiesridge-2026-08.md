Technical availability recovered to 96.0% (contractual 98.5%), with WTG61 (91.4%) the weakest turbine and PCS11 (77.9%) the weakest PCS unit (fleet average 88.2%). Net MWh stayed negative at -4,765 MWh penalty-adjusted, and the official average weighting factor was 1.35x. Total billed payment was R3.90 m at R288.13/MWh.
